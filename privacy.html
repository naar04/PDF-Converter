Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

This application ("SMM Automation") is a personal tool used to schedule posts to Facebook Pages. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Data Collection

We only collect the data you explicitly provide: CSV files for post content and Facebook Page Access Tokens via the official Facebook Login for Business flow.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used strictly for the purpose of scheduling and publishing posts to your selected Facebook Pages. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing your account from the dashboard or by contacting the developer. Users can also revoke app permissions directly through their Facebook Business Integrations settings.

4. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository.
